Delete your account

Last updated: 27 October 2026

You can delete your Spire Wars account and the data linked to it in two ways, or delete only some data and keep the account (see below).

1. In the game (immediate)

  1. Open Spire Wars and tap Online.
  2. If you are not signed in, log in with email and password.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your email and password again and confirm with Delete for ever.

2. Without the game (by email)

Write to axelfolie@gmail.com from your account's email address, with the subject “Spire Wars account deletion” and your player name. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm it. If you write from another address, we may ask you to prove the account is yours.

Delete only some data, keeping the account

You can ask us to erase only part of your data and keep the account. Write to axelfolie@gmail.com from your account's email address, with your player name and what you want deleted:

  • your places in the world rankings (your name, trophies and Adventure level no longer appear in the rankings);
  • progress saved online (trophies, league, records, coins, gems, matches played and won, Adventure, collection and achievements), which restarts from zero.

We carry out the request within 30 days and confirm it. You can delete the data saved on the phone yourself from Android Settings → Apps → Spire Wars → Storage → Clear data.

What is deleted

  • The account: email, player name and password.
  • Progress saved online: trophies, league, records, coins, gems, matches played and won, Adventure, collection and achievements.
  • Your places in the world rankings (online and Adventure).

What remains

  • Data saved on your phone (settings, coins, statistics of matches against the computer) stay until you uninstall the game or clear its data from Android Settings → Apps → Spire Wars → Storage.
  • Advertising data processed by Google follow Google's rules: you can manage them from Settings → Ad preferences in the game and from your Google account settings.
  • We keep no other copies of account data. The email you use to request deletion is kept only to show that the request was handled.

For other requests about your data see the privacy policy.

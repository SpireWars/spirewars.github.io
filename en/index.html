How to play

Easy to learn, hard to stop

  1. 1

    Towers train

    Every tower trains a soldier a second. The more towers you hold, the bigger your army.

  2. 2

    Found new towers

    Buy a Builder with gold and send it to a free cell: a new tower rises there.

  3. 3

    Lead the waves

    One tap on «All», one tap on the map: your soldiers march and fight everything they meet.

  4. 4

    Upgrade and defend

    Every enemy you defeat gives gold: make your army stronger and put soldiers in your towers to shoot arrows.

New

The tutorial takes you by the hand

Before level 1, a real guided match in 10 steps: a card tells you what to do, a golden arrow shows what to tap and the enemy waits until you are ready. Skip it, or replay it any time from «How to play».

  • 10 steps in a real match
  • Arrows on buttons, towers and cells
  • 50 coins on the first win
Adventure

A map that never ends

Follow the path level after level through six worlds that come back harder every time. Each level gives up to three stars, stars fill chests and every ten levels a boss is waiting.

  • Three stars per levelwin, keep your towers, lose few
  • Heartsfive tries that refill over time
  • Chestscoins, gems and skin pieces

Six worlds, six bosses

  • World 1 Meadow the Thornwood Warden
  • World 2 Desert the Sand Sultan
  • World 3 Red Canyon the Red Gorge Warlord
  • World 4 Swamp the Bog Witch
  • World 5 Snowlands the Frost Jarl
  • World 6 Volcano the Ash King
Bosses

Bring down the citadel

Every world ends with a named boss ruling from a citadel: double health, armor, archers on the walls and a lieutenant at its side. The music turns grim, the name glows above the walls… and the first win gives a gold chest and gems.

Special modes

Every level, a new challenge

Besides classic conquest, special levels change the rules: spot them on the map by their hexagonal gem.

  • Race against time

    Win before the time runs out.

  • War economy

    Nobody earns gold: spend the starting treasury well.

  • Fortress

    No builders: defend and upgrade the towers you have.

  • Magic shrines

    Glowing cells that give their tower a power.

  • Protect the king

    If the crowned castle falls, that army is out.

  • Hold the line

    Last until the end: every wave is stronger.

  • Treasure hunt

    Chests hidden in the fog: collect the most to win.

  • Total darkness

    The fog forgets: you see only what your soldiers see.

  • Everyone against you

    Three allied armies attack you together.

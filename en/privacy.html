Privacy policy

Privacy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy covers the game Spire Wars for Android (package com.axelproduction.spirewars) and this website. Under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) it explains which personal data are processed, by whom, why and for how long.

Controller and contact

Data controller: axelfolie, developer of the game under the Axel Production brand. Contact for any privacy request: axelfolie@gmail.com. No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed, as none is required.

In short

  • Playing against the computer needs no account: settings and progress stay on your phone.
  • Playing online needs an account (email, player name, password). Your player name, trophies and Adventure level are visible to other players in the world rankings; your name and actions also in online matches.
  • Online play goes through the GD-Sync service, which links the phones together and stores the accounts.
  • At the end of matches a video ad from Google AdMob may appear. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first, and you can change it at any time.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the game or by writing to us (how).
  • There are no in-app purchases and we do not sell your data.

Accounts and online play

The account is used only for online play. The password is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and stored by the service provider; the controller does not see it. A session code stays on the phone to keep you signed in for up to 30 days and is erased when you log out. During an online match the other players see your player name, your faction and your game actions. There is no chat.

Provider: GD-Sync (gd-sync.com), which processes the data on behalf of the controller (processor, Art. 28 GDPR) under its own terms and privacy policy. Staff of the provider and of the controller access data only when needed to run, protect or support the service.

Advertising and consent

At the end of each match a video ad provided by Google AdMob may appear. In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, before loading ads the game shows Google's consent form, a consent management platform certified for IAB Europe's Transparency & Consent Framework. You can review or withdraw your choices at any time from Settings → Ad preferences; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. If you refuse, non-personalised ads may appear, which still use some technical data, for example to limit frequency and fraud.

Google processes these data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from partner apps. Ads are limited to content suitable for ages 13 and up.

Recipients

Data are shared only with: GD-Sync (online service, as processor); Google (advertising, as an independent controller; Google Play for distribution, under its own terms); other players, limited to player name, trophies and in-match actions. We do not sell or transfer data to third parties for marketing.

Transfers outside the European Union

Google and GD-Sync may process data outside the European Economic Area. Any such transfer relies on the safeguards of Articles 44–49 GDPR, such as adequacy decisions (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Retention

  • Data on the phone: until you delete them or uninstall the game.
  • Account, online progress and ranking: as long as the account exists. Deleting the account in the game erases them from the service at once; if you ask by email, within 30 days of the request.
  • Technical data of online matches: only for the length of the match, except the provider's technical logs, kept as long as needed for the security of the service.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle the request and to show it was handled.
  • Data processed by Google: according to Google's retention periods.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, object to processing based on legitimate interest and withdraw consent at any time (Articles 15–22 GDPR) by writing to axelfolie@gmail.com. We answer within one month. To check that the request comes from you we may ask you to write from the account's email address. For data processed by Google you can also contact Google. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian supervisory authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or with the authority of the country where you live or work.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 14. Anyone under 14 must not create an online account without the consent of a parent or guardian. If we learn that we collected data of a child under 14 without such consent, we delete them; to report it, write to axelfolie@gmail.com.

Security

Communication with the online service is encrypted. The game accepts each player's commands only for their own army and compares the match state between phones to detect tampering. No system is 100% secure: if a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will inform you as the GDPR requires.

This website

The website uses no cookies, analytics, or third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical visit data such as the IP address for security reasons (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. Important changes are also announced in the game.
